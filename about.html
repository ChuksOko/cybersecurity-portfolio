Introduction

Professional biography

I'm Chukwuemeka Oko, a Junior SOC Analyst based in Port Harcourt, Nigeria. I hold a B.Sc. in Biochemistry from Abia State University, but my professional path took shape in the field — spending over five years supporting more than 100 production ATM terminals across South-South and South-East Nigeria. That operational grounding, where every fault had a business impact and every fix had to be documented, is what pulled me toward cybersecurity: the same discipline, applied to detecting and responding to threats instead of hardware faults. Today I split my time between hands-on SOC work at Bincom Dev Center, advanced practical training through the Ubuntu Bridge Initiative, and running CyberWatch NG, a platform I founded to bring cybersecurity awareness to everyday Nigerians.

Professional Philosophy

How I think about security

I believe effective cybersecurity begins with understanding how technology supports the business. My background in financial infrastructure taught me that every technical incident has an operational impact, while cybersecurity has taught me that every alert deserves evidence-based investigation. I enjoy building practical security solutions, improving detection capabilities, and translating complex security concepts into actionable guidance that strengthens organizational resilience.
