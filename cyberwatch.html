Community Initiative

CyberWatch NG

A digital-safety initiative I founded to make cybersecurity practical and accessible for Nigerians — helping individuals, businesses, and communities recognize and defend against evolving cyber threats.

Mission

To improve cybersecurity awareness across Nigeria by delivering practical, locally relevant education that helps individuals, businesses, and communities recognize and defend against evolving cyber threats.

Vision

To become Nigeria's most trusted community-driven cybersecurity education platform, empowering people with the knowledge needed to build a safer digital society.

Objectives

What we're working toward

  • Promote cybersecurity awareness
  • Simplify technical concepts for non-technical audiences
  • Publish threat alerts as they emerge
  • Support students entering cybersecurity
  • Build a security-conscious digital community
Why It Exists

The gap CyberWatch NG fills

Cyber threats targeting Nigerians — from phishing and social engineering to banking scams — are rising, while cybersecurity education remains largely inaccessible to the people most affected. CyberWatch NG exists to close that gap: turning real attack patterns into plain-language guidance so individuals and small businesses can protect themselves without needing a technical background.
